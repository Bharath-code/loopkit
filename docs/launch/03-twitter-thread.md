# Twitter / X Launch Thread

> **Format:** 7 tweets. 280 chars max each. Numbered 1/.
> **Post time:** Tuesday or Wednesday, 9–11am PT.
> **First tweet is the hook** — the rest is proof, not pitch.
> **Pin to profile for 48h.**

## Thread

**1/ (hook)**
> I kept quitting side projects at week 4.
>
> Same project, 4 times. Different ideas, same pattern.
>
> So I built the thing I needed: a CLI that runs a weekly shipping loop and breaks the streak if I miss a Sunday.
>
> It's called LoopKit. It's free. ↓

**2/ (the pattern)**
> The pattern was always the same:
>
> 1. Build hard for a month
> 2. Hit a phase that requires a different energy (writing, distribution, sales)
> 3. Quietly stop
> 4. Move to next idea
>
> Not because the ideas were bad. Because the *loop* was hard to hold in my head.

**3/ (what it is)**
> LoopKit is 5 commands:
>
> • `init` — turn an idea into a falsifiable brief in 4 min
> • `track` — markdown tasks, git commits close them
> • `ship` — AI writes your launch post
> • `pulse` — async feedback, AI-clustered
> • `loop` — Sunday ritual. One decision. Streak starts.
>
> All plain text. All in your repo. No SaaS lock-in.

**4/ (the killer feature)**
> The thing I'm most proud of is `loop`.
>
> It runs AI synthesis on what you shipped, asks you the ONE hard question ("what's the thing holding you back?"), generates a build-in-public post, and starts a streak counter.
>
> Skip a Sunday = streak breaks. The pressure is gentle but real.

**5/ (real numbers)**
> Some numbers, since people will ask:
>
> • 626 automated tests
> • 13,700+ lines (CLI + web + shared)
> • 4 min from `npx` to first brief
> • 0 data collected without opt-in
> • $0 free tier covers the full weekly loop
>
> Paid tier (Solo $19, Pro $39) only needed if you want hosted AI.

**6/ (who it's for)**
> Built for solo technical founders who already use a terminal and a git repo.
>
> If you have a Notion graveyard, 4 half-shipped side projects, and "I'll write the launch post tomorrow" on your todo list for 6 weeks — this is for you.
>
> If you have a team and use Jira, this is not for you. (Yet.)

**7/ (CTA)**
> `npx loopkit init`
>
> That's the install. 4 minutes to your first brief.
>
> Looking for 10 founders to try it for 4 weeks. If it doesn't change how you ship, I'll refund you.
>
> Show HN post is up too. RT the first tweet if this resonated 🧵
